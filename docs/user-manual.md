## Pay with Maya
1. Finalize the sale. If money is still owed, the green message offers **pay the balance with Maya**. You can also open **Reports & Search → Transactions** and click **Pay with Maya** on the sale.
2. Check the amount and click **Create Maya checkout**.
3. Show the customer the **QR code**, or click **Open Maya checkout** to pay on this device. The customer pays on Maya's page.
4. This page updates by itself when Maya confirms. The sale then shows **Paid**. If it does not update, click **Check status now**.
5. If the checkout fails, expires or is cancelled, nothing is charged: click **Create a new checkout**.
All Maya payments are listed under **Maya Payments** in the sidebar.
